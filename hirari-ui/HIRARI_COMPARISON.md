# Hirari capability comparison

Hirari is a technical preview and is not feature-equivalent to Cubase Pro 15.
The current, evidence-based comparison and work order lives in the
[Cubase parity roadmap](../docs/CUBASE_PARITY_ROADMAP.md).

The previous Ardour table compared lines of code, memory, and binary size
without reproducible measurement procedures or matching build configurations.
Those figures do not establish capability, performance, reliability, or
engineering efficiency and are withdrawn.

## Comparison standard

- Compare user workflows: record, edit, mix, save, reopen, and export.
- Separate features visible in the UI from engine contracts, automated checks,
  and real-device or third-party verification.
- Do not use source-code size, implementation language, or open-source status
  as evidence of audio quality or feature parity.
- Keep licensing information factual and separate from product capability
  claims. Hirari's license may change independently of the parity roadmap.
